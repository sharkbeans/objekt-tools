What all three share

White is the only accent

Every surface, border and label is a true grey with no tint. Buttons, selection and focus are white on near-black.

Objekt art keeps its colour

The cards are the content, so they stay in full colour. The interface around them stays out of the way.

Fill replaces colour on the desk

Today the match desk uses green for your side and the primary colour for theirs. Here a solid badge means they want your card 4W and an outlined one means they have it 2H.

Every face is free on Google Fonts

Every family here is open source and loads through next/font. Each set includes a mono for serials, counts and Seoul time.
