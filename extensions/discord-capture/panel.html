Before you start

  • Reads the name, text and time of posts in channels you turn on, and in searches you run.
  • Keeps them in this browser.
  • Sends them to objekt.my/match only when you press Open in match — they stay in your browser there too.
  • Gets card pictures for your wants, and your inventory if you add a Cosmo nickname, from objekt.my.
  • Never reads your Discord token, calls Discord's API, or posts anything.

Read this first: capture reads the visible posts in every channel you enable, including other people's names and messages. Captured content may include personal or sensitive information. Only turn it on in channels where you are allowed to collect and handle those posts.

Other people's posts are their words: keep them to yourself. Privacy policy

Open a trade channel in Discord

    Search types your codes into Discord's search box for you. That is automation, which Discord's terms do not allow, and Discord may action accounts for it.
