Before you start

  • Reads the name, text and time of posts in channels you turn on, and in searches you run.
  • Keeps them in this browser.
  • Sends them to objekt.my/match only when you press Open in match — they stay in your browser there too.
  • Gets card pictures for your wants, and your inventory if you add a Cosmo nickname, from objekt.my.
  • Never reads your Discord token, calls Discord's API, or posts anything.

Other people's posts are their words: keep them to yourself. Privacy policy
