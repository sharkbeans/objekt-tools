Before you start

  • Reads the name, text and time of posts in the server channels you open, and in searches you run. Capture is on in every server channel until you pause it there. Direct messages are never read.
  • Keeps them in this browser.
  • Sends them to objekt.my/match only when you press Open in match.
  • Gets card pictures for your wants, and your inventory if you add a Cosmo nickname, from objekt.my.
  • Never reads your Discord token, calls Discord's API, or posts anything.

Read this first: capture reads the visible posts in every server channel you open, including other people's names and messages, without asking again per channel. Captured content may include personal or sensitive information. Pause it in any channel where you are not allowed to collect and handle those posts.

Other people's posts are their words: keep them to yourself. Privacy policy

No channel

    Search types your codes into Discord's search box for you. That is automation, which Discord's terms do not allow, and Discord may action accounts for it.

    Searching paused for 10:00

    Discord stopped answering your searches. That is what a rate limit looks like, and it means Discord has noticed this account searching quickly.
    • Searching again now makes it more likely Discord locks, flags or bans your account. This extension cannot undo that.
    • Waiting out the timer is the safe choice. Searches that start too early usually just stall again.
    • If you go on, use a slow pace (5s or more) and fewer pages.

    Settings

    Lower delays are faster, but may trigger Discord rate limits and a forced wait.

    Results come newest first, and old trade posts are mostly gone. Stopping early sends Discord fewer requests. A code searched before also stops as soon as a page holds nothing new.
