Before you start

This extension reads Discord posts you can already see, so you need to agree to that first. Here is exactly what it does:

  • Collects the author name, text and timestamp of messages in the channels you switch on — nothing else, and only while you are looking at them.
  • Stores them in this browser (IndexedDB), on your device.
  • Sends nothing anywhere on its own. Exports are files you download and hand to objekt.my/match yourself.
  • Optionally asks objekt.my for the objekts owned by a Cosmo nickname you type. That is the only network request, and only when you press the button.
  • Never reads your Discord token, calls Discord's API, or sends anything to Discord.

Full detail: privacy policy. Other people's posts are their words — keep the export to yourself and delete it when you are done.
